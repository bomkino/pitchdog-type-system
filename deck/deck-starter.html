How it scales

Type is sized by the slide, not by the screen.

Every deck role is measured in container units, so a slide sets the same way on a laptop, a projector and a PDF.

A 16:9 slide sets type from its height. A 4:3 slide is narrower, so the same role lands at 80 percent of its widescreen size, and copy held to a measure keeps its line breaks.
