# Research sources

## Web wrapping and reading measure

Checked 31 August 2026.

- W3C CSS Text Module Level 4 — normative definitions and intended jobs for `balance`, `pretty`, `stable` and `avoid-orphans`.
- MDN `text-wrap-style` — current cross-browser availability, performance guidance and browser-specific balance limits.
- W3C WAI Understanding WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.8 — the 80-character line-length ceiling and the accessibility cost of long lines.
- U.S. Web Design System Typography — a 45–90 character working range and a 66-character target for long reading.
- GOV.UK Design System Layout — a practical 75-character recommendation for ordinary page layouts.

## YouTube extension

Checked 28 August 2026.

- YouTube Help, “Add custom thumbnails on YouTube” — recommended video, Shorts and podcast-cover dimensions and aspect ratios.
- YouTube Help, “Thumbnail & title tips” — cross-device readability, simple composition, readable fonts, accurate and succinct titles, and thumbnail/title experimentation.
- YouTube Help, “Manage your channel branding” — banner minimum, recommended size and central text/logo safe area.
- YouTube Help, “Add end screens to videos” — final 5–20 seconds and up to four elements for standard 16:9 videos.
- YouTube Help, “A/B test titles and thumbnails” — high-resolution requirement and meaningful variant testing.

## Subtitles

Checked 29 September 2026.

- BBC Subtitle Guidelines — section 3.1 line length (68 % of a 16:9 frame's width, 90 % of 4:3, 1:1 and 9:16), 9.2.1 line height (7–8 % of video height for 16:9, 4:3 and 1:1; 3.9–4.5 % for 9:16) and 9.2.4 a background behind each line with no gap between lines.
- Netflix Timed Text Style Guide, General Requirements — five-sixths of a second to seven seconds per event, two lines at most, centred at the top or bottom of the frame.
- Netflix English (USA) Timed Text Style Guide — 42 characters per line, bottom-heavy line breaks and where to break, dual speakers with a hyphen and no space, when to italicise, and 20 (adult) or 17 (children) characters per second.
- W3C WebVTT — the properties a page may set on `::cue`.
- Third-party safe-zone guides for Instagram Reels and TikTok — a column of buttons along the right edge, about 11 % of the width, and the caption and audio credit across the bottom 17 to 35 % of the height. They are not platform documentation, so the vertical cue (30 % up, 76 % wide) is the system's own choice inside their ranges. Both apps move their interface; recheck before relying on it.

## Delivery sizes

Checked 29 September 2026. These are working estimates the system uses to size small text, not platform specifications.

- Social feed posts: a 1080-pixel-wide post shows about 390 px wide in a phone feed.
- YouTube thumbnails: a 3840 × 2160 thumbnail is seen at roughly 10–15 % of its size in search and suggestions.
