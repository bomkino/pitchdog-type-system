# Social-media typography

Social media is a governed output layer over the same five voices.

## Canvases

- `square` — 1080 × 1080; Feed statement, quote, proof point or launch cover
- `portrait` — 1080 × 1350; Primary feed post and carousel slide
- `story` — 1080 × 1920; Story and reel cover with interface-chrome protection
- `landscape` — 1200 × 630; Open Graph, LinkedIn and link preview

## Roles

- `social.display` — head 600; maximum 4 lines / 12 words
- `social.headline` — head 500; maximum 4 lines / 18 words
- `social.subhead` — bodyAlt 400; maximum 6 lines / 32 words
- `social.body` — body 400; maximum 10 lines / 62 words
- `social.quote` — head 500; maximum 7 lines / 38 words
- `social.metric` — body 700; maximum 2 lines / 4 words
- `social.label` — body 600; maximum 2 lines / 10 words
- `social.metadata` — eyebrow 500; maximum 3 lines / 18 words
- `social.credit` — body 600; maximum 2 lines / 14 words

## Rules

- One dominant message per frame.
- Move detail into the caption or next carousel slide.
- Never shrink type to rescue excessive copy.
- Keep essential text inside the safe region.
- Alt text accompanies informative images.
- Spoken video receives captions.
- Final compressed posts receive a physical-phone review.
- Social introduces no additional typeface.
